About the Author

Adrian Cruz

Adrian Cruz is an independent children's book author creating warm, imaginative stories for young readers and families. His books pair playful adventures with ideas children can understand—from computers, artificial intelligence, choices, and privacy to friendship, confidence, emotions, worry, and growing up.

His books are designed to be approachable for children while giving parents, caregivers, and educators a natural starting point for bigger conversations.

For Parents & Educators

Read the story. Keep the conversation going.

Whether the topic is technology, privacy, confidence, friendship, worry, or making a hard choice, Adrian's books are designed to give children a story first and a conversation worth continuing after the last page.
